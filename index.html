Cyclone Murasu (Bay of Bengal): WRF hindcast for the Andhra coast and Tirupati

Research simulation, HYDROS Lab, IIT Tirupati. WRF 9 km / 3 km started from NOAA GFS of 1 Nov 2026, 12 UTC (17:30 IST), run for 60 hours to 4 Nov, 00 UTC (05:30 IST).

This is not a forecast. The run was done after the event and compared with the IMD bulletin issued at 17:30 IST on 1 Nov, the same time as the model start. For warnings and decisions use the India Meteorological Department: mausam.imd.gov.in.

Where the model takes the system

Show:

Red line: model cyclone centre (lowest sea level pressure); dots every 3 hours, large dots at 00 UTC. Tan diamonds: IMD analysed position at the start and IMD forecast positions (bulletin No. 8). Rain and wind are from the 9 km domain, regridded to 0.25 degrees; totals can be large near the coast because the vortex is strong, they are not point values for a place.

The model starts on top of the IMD position and then moves about 50 to 55 km to the south-east of it, a little slower, for the first 36 hours. In the model the centre is still just offshore at 05:30 IST on 3 Nov (80.4E) and reaches the coast around 08:30 IST, a few hours after the IMD landfall window (between 23:30 IST on 2 Nov and 04:00 IST on 3 Nov, between Nellore and Narsapur near Ongole, IMD forecast; the landfall time is read from the model track). After landfall the model system stays near 80.4E and fills (1007 hPa by 05:30 IST on 4 Nov) while IMD takes it north-west, so the distance grows to 100 and then 195 km.

IMD positions are from the forecast table of IMD National Bulletin No. 8, issued 1 Nov 2026 17:30 IST (pdf); later IMD bulletins with the observed track have not been used yet. Distances are rounded to 5 km. Reports say the storm crossed the coast near Ongole in the early hours of 3 Nov at 100 to 110 km/h (news report: BusinessToday, 3 Nov 2026).

What it means for Tirupati

Rain is the mean of 3 km cells within 25 km of the campus (bars) with the single campus cell in the table; wind is the highest 10 m wind within 25 km. IMD gridded rainfall is the value of the 0.25 degree cell containing the campus; IMD days run 08:30 to 08:30 IST, so IMD 3 Nov contains the afternoon and night of 2 Nov. The run ends at 05:30 IST on 4 Nov, so its last day is partial (21 h). IMD records 0.0 mm for 4 Nov (3 Nov 08:30 to 4 Nov 08:30 IST), while the model campus cell shows 10 mm from isolated showers on the afternoon of 3 Nov; the 25 km mean is only 1.0 mm, so a single cell should not be read as a forecast for the campus. On 2 Nov the model puts the rain band over the area between 12:30 and 14:30 IST, which agrees with the afternoon rain reported on campus, but the 25 km mean is about half of what IMD records (one event, so it says little about the general bias).

Limits

One starting field (GFS), 9 km outer grid, no data assimilation, no bogus vortex, Kain–Fritsch convection on both grids. One storm, one run: it shows what this configuration did for Murasu, not how it will do next time. The highest model wind (126 km/h) is somewhat above the IMD landfall value (100 to 110 km/h, gusts 120), but the two are different kinds of wind, and the model landfall is a few hours later than the IMD window.
