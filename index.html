Bay of Bengal cyclone: WRF outlook for Tirupati

Research simulation, HYDROS lab, IIT Tirupati. Run 1: WRF 9 km / 3 km started from NOAA GFS of 25 Oct 2026, 12 UTC (17:30 IST). Prepared 26 Oct 2026 for internal review.

This is not a forecast. It is one model run, not yet checked against observations, and its track differs from the IMD forecast. For warnings and decisions use the India Meteorological Department: mausam.imd.gov.in and the Amaravati / Chennai centre bulletins.

Where the model takes the system

Dots on the track are every 3 hours (UTC); large dots mark 00 UTC with the date. Rain and wind are from the 9 km domain, regridded to 0.25 degrees; daily totals can be large over the sea and near the coast because the model vortex is strong, they are not point values for a place.

Intensity over time

Highest 10 m wind found anywhere over the sea part of the 9 km domain (the lowest pressure is in the boxes at the top). Dashed lines are IMD thresholds (cyclonic storm 34 kt, very severe 64 kt, super cyclone 120 kt = 62 m/s). The first 12 to 24 hours are model spin-up from a weak starting vortex.

What it means for Tirupati in this run

Rain is the mean of 3 km cells within 25 km of the campus (bars) with the single campus cell in the table; wind is the highest 10 m wind within 25 km. Light rain of a few mm per day would still be possible; the model shows no heavy rain at Tirupati.

Run 2 and what is still open

Run 2 starts from the GFS of 26 Oct, 00 UTC, and is running on the HPC now (finishes tonight). It will be added here, so track and landfall time can be compared with Run 1 and with the IMD bulletins.

Limits: one starting field (GFS), 9 km outer grid, no data assimilation, no bogus vortex. In this run the centre appears to reach the north Andhra / Odisha coast around 29 Oct, about a day later than the IMD forecast (landfall between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam on the forenoon of 28 Oct), which shows how sensitive the result is to the starting vortex.

Model: WRF 4.6.1. Starting data: NOAA/NCEP GFS 0.25 deg. Official cyclone information: India Meteorological Department.
